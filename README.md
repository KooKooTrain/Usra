# Usra أسرة - Family App

Usra (أسرة) quite literally means *family* in Arabic, and thats exactly what our app is based on.

Usra is a modern family management and wellbeing application designed to solve **real-world family challenges** through technology, communication, safety, and organization.

The app was built around genuine day-to-day concerns faced by families [Refer to images below], focusing on improving coordination, safety, healthcare management, and connected living through one unified platform.

# Why We Chose a Mobile App Instead of a Website

Apps offer a more convenient, secure, and personalized experience that leverages **smartphone hardware**, such as GPS for location sharing and push notifications for instant alerts.

Families require fast, real-time accessibility throughout the day, especially during emergencies, travel, scheduling, and communication. A mobile application provides instant notifications, location integration, portability compared to a traditional website.

**For our prototype, we made a mobile view on web to make it easily accessible for judges without the need of an app emulator or mobile device.**

# Real-World Research & Survey

Usra was designed after studying real-world family concerns and common day-to-day problems faced by modern households. We also conducted surveys and gathered user insights to better understand the needs of parents, children, and elderly family members.

The features and modules inside Usra were carefully planned around practical use cases and realistic family situations.

# Core Features

![Usra AI](https://res.cloudinary.com/dw4mv7p40/image/upload/v1778692909/38a9fe9a-18fb-43a8-9cb5-1106ce888efd.png)

## 1. Safety

A real-time family safety system that allows members to:
- View whether family members are safe
- Share live location updates
- Trigger emergency alerts
- Notify trusted family members during danger or emergencies
- Emergency numbers contacted in case of any SOS Signal
---

## 2. Shared Calendar

A collaborative family calendar designed to improve organization and planning by allowing:
- Shared schedules
- Event reminders
- Appointment tracking
- Family activity planning
- Automatic display of important occasions and cultural events such as Ramadan, Eid, etc.

---

## 3. Budget Tracker

A finance management system that helps families:
- Track spending
- Plan budgets together
- Manage shared expenses
- Improve financial organization

---

# Additional Features

## Family Chat
A dedicated communication space for family conversations, updates, and coordination.

---

## Health Hub

A healthcare management module that includes:

- Regular health checkup reminders

## Kids Module

Focused on child growth and digital wellbeing:

## Elder Care Hub

A support system designed for elderly family members:
- Medicine reminders
- Appointment management
- Memory and routine support
- Health-related assistance

# Vision

Usra aims to create a connected digital ecosystem for families by combining safety, communication, healthcare, organization, and intelligent assistance into one unified platform.

The goal is to simplify family life while strengthening coordination, awareness, and wellbeing for every member of the household.
